Design direction for the invitation cover.

The cover should feel like a physical editorial wedding stationery piece: solid paper, watercolor texture, embossed floral details, quiet typography, and a soft gatefold opening.

Visual Language

  • Solid paper objects rather than glass or transparent vellum.
  • Sage, ivory, powder blue, charcoal, and warm black.
  • Embossed or debossed floral artwork with soft natural shadows.
  • Laser-cut or die-cut floral panel edges.
  • Minimal serif typography with generous negative space.
  • Handwritten script as a small accent only.

Animation

  • Left and right panels rotate open from their outer edges.
  • The monogram seal fades and scales away first.
  • The inner invitation rises after the panels begin opening.
  • Motion should feel tactile, slow, and soft.
